# Brand Identity and Store Copy — FLC Decor

**Official names supplied by owner:** Storefront **Fahad Laser**; company/brand **FLC Decor**. Do not change these without approval.

## Proposed positioning (copy subject to owner approval)
**Motto:** *Crafted with Precision. Designed to Inspire.*  
**Main store line:** *Turn Your Walls Into a Statement.*  
**Supporting line:** *Precision-Cut Decor for Beautiful Spaces.*

**Aim:** To help customers in Pakistan create more expressive homes, workspaces and gifts through attractive laser-cut decorative pieces.

**Purpose:** To make discovering, selecting and ordering decorative pieces simple and inspiring, combining detailed product presentation, transparent variant choices and dependable order management.

**Mission:** *Bring distinctive decorative details into everyday spaces through thoughtful designs and a convenient shopping experience.*

**Vision:** *Become a recognizable destination in Pakistan for creative, accessible laser-cut decor.*

Product categories and materials must come from real catalog data. Contact number, WhatsApp link and social accounts must be configured by owner.
