The Architecture of Space

Laser-Sculpted Form Meets Raw Organic Timber.

Rooted in centuries of historic Mughal archway proportions and distilled through Scandinavian restraint, The Mihrab Silhouette panel casts changing geometric shadows as sunlight traverses your living room.

Every curve is cut with an industrial-grade CO2 laser bed operating at sub-millimeter tolerances in our Lahore atelier. After laser profiling, the timber is hand-burnished, coated in beeswax polish, and joined with a warm champagne brass reflector plane.

6.2 mm Solid structural relief depth for distinct shadow projection
100% Concealed hardware with zero front-facing penetrations

• Top Layer: 6mm Premium American Walnut architectural grade veneer with natural open-pore wood grain.

• Inner Core: High-density moisture-resistant architectural substrate preventing warping in seasonal humidity shifts.

• Sub-Plate Reflector: Hand-brushed champagne brass alloy treated with anti-tarnish micro-lacquer.

• Finishing: Two coats of Italian non-toxic organic beeswax, hand-buffed to a soft matte gleam.

• Designed for effortless self-installation within 15 minutes.

• Features an interlocking aluminum French cleat system rated to hold up to 35 kg securely on concrete, brick, or drywall.

• Includes 1:1 scale paper alignment template with built-in magnetic bubble level, Fischer wall plugs, and high-tensile anchor screws.
