## Brand & Style

This design system embodies "Quiet Luxury Meets Modern Minimalism," tailored for an artisanal home décor atelier specializing in intricate laser-cut metalwork, warm timber accents, and modern spatial artistry. The emotional resonance is calm, architectural, discerning, and deeply tactile—evoking the feeling of browsing an unhurried, curated architectural monograph or an haute-couture design gallery.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Precision**:
- **Uncompromised Breathing Room:** Generous negative space and asymmetric editorial compositions prioritize object silhouettes over cluttered visual density.
- **Architectural Proportion:** Precision alignment, ultra-fine 1px hairline dividers, and controlled geometry celebrate the laser-cut craft without visual noise.
- **Understated Materiality:** Soft stone palettes, frosted translucent glass, warm taupe accents, and deep charcoal noir contrast against clean ivory planes.

## Colors

The palette avoids stark sterile whites and loud consumer accents, leaning into warm natural stones, patinated metals, and vegetal undertones.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary Canvas (`#F8F6F2` - Warm Ivory):** The primary backdrop, reducing eye fatigue and evoking textured museum-grade cotton paper.
- **Secondary Surfaces & Containers (`#FFFFFF` & `#F3EFEA`):** Pure white is reserved strictly for focal product cutouts, hovering drawer panels, and primary card backgrounds. `#F3EFEA` (Warm Stone) serves as a grounding tertiary container for grouped modules and filter trays.
- **Primary Typography & Contrast (`#1A1A1A` - Charcoal Noir & `#242424`):** Delivers resolute contrast without the clinical harshness of `#000000`.
- **Craftsmanship Accent (`#B39B7D` - Brass Gold & `#A79A8A` - Warm Taupe):** Applied selectively to interactive active states, gold foil badges, metadata keylines, and highlight rules.
- **Botanical Accent (`#929C8B` - Muted Sage):** Subdued organic tone used exclusively for sustainability notes, artisan certifications, in-stock micro-indicators, and botanical collection tagging.
- **Hairlines & Boundaries (`#EAE6DF` - Light Warm Stone):** Subtle dividers that frame modules without breaking compositional continuity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast editorial elegance with hyper-legible, modern e-commerce utility.

- **Display & Headings:** Set in *Playfair Display*. Utilized with ample line-height and tight tracking on heroic headers to emphasize the architectural, artisanal nature of the brand.
- **Body & Product Information:** Set in *Plus Jakarta Sans*. Delivers optical clarity for dimensions, wood grain classifications, metal finishing grades, and logistics.
- **Eyebrows & Sub-headers:** Rendered in uppercase *Plus Jakarta Sans* with wide letter-spacing (`0.18em`), anchoring editorial collection narratives and category badges.
- **Currency & Pricing:** Formatted with *Plus Jakarta Sans* semibold numbers; the currency sign `PKR` is set as an uppercase muted label with a slight horizontal offset to maintain typographic balance without overshadowing the amount.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column architectural grid engineered for rhythmic editorial storytelling and fluid shopping experiences.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout; canvas margin `margin: 1.25rem`; column gutter `gutter: 1.5rem`. Modules stack with distinct vertical breathing room (`space-2xl`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout; canvas margin `margin-tablet: 2.5rem`; column gutter `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout; outer canvas margin `margin-desktop: 5rem`; column gutter `gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`; max content width capped at `1440px` centered.

### Rhythm & Composition
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Feature spotlights utilize a 7:5 or 8:4 column split between full-height lifestyle photography and minimal typographic product specs.
- **Vertical Modulation:** Major narrative transitions deploy `space-3xl` (6rem), avoiding dense block stacking. Micro-interactions and card metadata respect an internal 4px/8px incremental rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile, warm ambient shadows and frosted surface transitions rather than harsh synthetic drops.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Flat Ground):** Base Ivory canvas (`#F8F6F2`). Zero shadow; separated strictly via 1px `#EAE6DF` hairline borders.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Curations):** White or Warm Stone containers. Elevation: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.03), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.02)`. Barely perceptible, providing tactile lift reminiscent of layered linen mats.
- **Tier 2 (Hovered Product Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward flotation. Elevation: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Drawer Sheets & Cart Overlays):** Elevation: `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.10)`. Accompanied by a 24% opacity charcoal backdrop overlay (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.24)` with `8px` background blur).

### Translucency & Frosted Glass
Sticky navigation headers and contextual action pills utilize a frosted glass formulation:
`background: rgba(248, 246, 242, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(140%); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(234, 230, 223, 0.7);`

## Shapes

The geometric personality reflects the intersection of architectural discipline and handcrafted furniture. The default roundedness is soft and restrained (`1` / `0.25rem`).

- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Standard `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This slight softening keeps imagery structured and architectural, honoring clean laser-cut lines while avoiding harsh utilitarian points.
- **Modals & Drawers:** `0.5rem` (8px) on inner cards; floating cart sheets feature `0.75rem` (12px) only on top or inward-facing corners.
- **Buttons & Badges:** Primary interactive CTAs adopt either a tailored `0.25rem` radius or an intentional full pill (`9999px`) strictly for floating contextual actions, quick-filter chips, and artisanal badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Charcoal Noir (`#1A1A1A`) fill, pure white text, 48px height, `0.25rem` radius. Padding: `0 28px`. Typography: `label-caps`. Hover: Background shifts to `#2E2A27` with a subtle +1px tactile lift.
- **Secondary (Craftsman Outline):** Transparent fill, 1px solid `#1A1A1A` hairline border, `#1A1A1A` text. Hover: Solid `#F3EFEA` fill with smooth 200ms ease.
- **Tertiary (Brass Accent Pill):** Light Brass Gold background (`rgba(179, 155, 125, 0.12)`), text in `#B39B7D`, 1px border (`#B39B7D`), pill radius (`9999px`).

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 3:4 aspect ratio imagery enclosed within a `#FFFFFF` card, subtle `1px solid #EAE6DF` outer rule. 
- **Metadata Grouping:** Image top-aligned; bottom segment carries an uppercase eyebrow (e.g., "HAND-PATINATED BRASS"), followed by the product title in Playfair Display (`headline-sm`), and PKR price (`price-sm`) formatted as `PKR 14,500`.
- **Interactive State:** Hover activates a secondary lifestyle image dissolve transition (400ms ease) and reveals a soft bottom "Quick View" floating pill drawer.

### Product Badges & Chips
- **Artisan / Handcrafted Badge:** Muted Ivory background (`#F8F6F2`), 1px solid `#B39B7D`, text `#B39B7D`, uppercase tracking (`0.12em`), 22px height, pill radius.
- **In-Stock Indicator:** Soft Sage tint (`rgba(146, 156, 139, 0.15)`), text `#4D5648`, preceded by a 5px solid circular dot (`#929C8B`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Minimalist underlined baseline or fully framed soft-stone box (`#FFFFFF`), 48px height, 1px border `#EAE6DF`. Typography: `body-md`. Focus state shifts the border to `#1A1A1A` without glowing halos.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted proportions (18px × 18px), 1px border `#B39B7D`. Active states fill with `#1A1A1A` housing an ultra-thin white tick mark.

### Floating Cart & Slide-Over Drawer
- Positioned on the right viewport edge, width: 440px max. Features frosted glass header, hairline separator, itemized product listings with timber/finish swatch pills, PKR subtotal calculations, and a high-contrast checkout CTA anchored at the base.
